Service model

Expert eSIM is designed as a sponsor-funded service. The operator intends to pay the contracted connectivity provider for eligible subscriber access; users are not asked to buy data packages through the current app. Each registered device is intended to receive its own separate eSIM profile. A profile is not an internet connection by itself: it must be issued by an authorised eSIM provisioning provider, installed on a compatible device, and activated by the mobile connectivity provider.

Coverage and unlimited-data description

“Global” describes the intended roaming service model, not a promise that every country, territory, operator, radio band or device is supported. Availability depends on the contracted provider's current coverage list, roaming agreements, local rules, device compatibility, network availability and any restrictions imposed by a visited network. The service will only be described as unlimited where the provider's binding contract and actual subscription response confirm an unlimited-data entitlement. Fair-use policies, speed management, tethering restrictions, permanent-roaming limits, blocked destinations and other conditions must be disclosed before activation.

Identity, device compatibility and activation

Users must provide accurate account and device details. The connectivity provider or a verification partner may require identity or eligibility checks in certain destinations or for certain subscription types. Those checks must use an authorised process appropriate to the market; Expert eSIM will not claim that one identity process applies in every country. A device needs compatible eUICC/eSIM hardware and software. Installation, activation and active mobile-data service are separate events and will only be shown as complete after the relevant system or provider confirms them.

Acceptable use, suspension and service limits

Users must not use the service for unlawful activity, fraud, network abuse or attempts to bypass technical restrictions. We or the connectivity provider may suspend or restrict a profile where required by law, a network partner, security controls or the service terms. Before launch, Expert eSIM must publish its detailed acceptable-use, fair-use, cancellation, profile replacement, outage and complaint procedures.

Service availability and changes

Mobile speeds and availability depend on the visited network, radio technology, signal, congestion, device support, location and provider routing. 4G and 5G are not guaranteed in every location. The operator will communicate material changes to the service and its coverage terms where required.
